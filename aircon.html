A clean unit cools better.

Dust on the coils and blower makes the unit work harder for less cold air. A proper cleaning gets the airflow back to where it should be. Kapag mainit na naman, you feel the difference.

  • Airflow feels weaker than it used to.
  • The unit smells musty when it starts.
  • Water drips from the indoor unit.
  • Dust is visible on the blower or coils.
  • The room takes longer to cool.
Book an aircon cleaning

What a service visit covers.

A cleaning is not just a filter rinse. This is the scope we work through, adapted to the unit and what it needs.

01

Filters and intake

Washed and checked. Clogged filters are a common cause of weak airflow.

02

Evaporator coils

Cleaned to remove the dust and film that reduce heat transfer.

03

Blower wheel

Dust build-up on the blower is cleared so air moves freely.

04

Drain pan and line

Drainage checked for clogs, slow flow, and leaks.

05

Refrigerant pressure

Pressure tested when cooling performance is in question.

06

Electrical connections

Loose or worn connections checked at the unit and controls.

07

Thermostat and controls

Set points, remote, and sensors checked for correct operation.

08

Mounting and brackets

Indoor and outdoor units checked for a secure, level fit.

Ask a question
How often should an aircon be cleaned?

It depends on dust, use, and the room. A unit in a busy or dusty space will need attention sooner. A technician can advise after checking the unit.

What should I prepare before the visit?

Clear access to the indoor and outdoor units. If you have the remote, manual, or model number, keep it nearby. Tell us about any error code you see.

Do you repair both split and window units?

We handle split, window, and floor-mounted units. Tell us the type when you book so the right tools come along.

Can you check a unit that is still under warranty?

Yes. Tell us the brand and warranty status when you book. For our authorized brands, we can advise on the right next step.

What happens if a part needs replacing?

The technician explains what is needed and the cost before any work continues. Work starts with your approval.

Do you install new units?

Yes. We assess the site, mount the unit, and check drainage and operation before we finish.

Let us get the cold back.

Tell us the unit type and what it is doing. We will take it from there.
